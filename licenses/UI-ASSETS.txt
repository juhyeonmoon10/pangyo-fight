Pangyo Fight approved 16-bit arcade interface / 2026-11-05

The user approved pangyo-sf2-select-concept.png and pangyo-sf2-battle-concept.png.
assets/fighters/hood-16bit.png and uniform-16bit.png are unmodified production
portraits generated with built-in imagegen from the approved selection concept.
They face right and left respectively. Matching *-16bit-face.png files are
technical face crops. SF2-ART-NOTES.txt, SF2-ASSET-REPORT.json, and the adjacent
SF2-HOOD-PROMPT.txt / SF2-UNIFORM-PROMPT.txt preserve production provenance.
The native school-stage artwork was generated from the approved battle concept
with fighters and HUD removed; its exact built-in imagegen prompt is preserved
as SF2-SCHOOL-STAGE-PROMPT.txt. The web shell does not display that stage PNG.
Lifeuniform, Ryu, and Sakura face assets retain the existing fighter identities,
resized with nearest-neighbor sampling. No new gameplay or selection state is
represented by the decorative launch roster.

Current web fonts:
- Press Start 2P, official Google Fonts repository, OFL 1.1.
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/pressstart2p
  License: PRESSSTART-OFL.txt.
- Galmuri11 Regular and Bold, official quiple/galmuri repository, OFL 1.1.
  https://github.com/quiple/galmuri
  License: GALMURI-OFL.txt.

The following records also cover older retained assets; Bangers and Black Han
Sans are no longer referenced by the current web-shell stylesheet.

Student portraits and face crops in assets/fighters/ are existing original
generated artwork from the previous uniform/hoodie update. Built-in imagegen
was used for that artwork; exact prompts and source PNGs are preserved in
pangyo-student-art-v2.zip. No student photographs are shipped.

lifeuniform-face.png, ryu-face.png and sakura-face.png are extracted from each
included fighter's SFF 9000,0 sprite. Original fighter credits and reuse terms
remain in CONTENT.txt. Character selection is individual one-versus-one.

The web interface is authored HTML/CSS geometry and text. In-game interface
graphics are generated from data/pangyo/make-art.ps1 and build-ui.mjs.
Original IKEMEN interface/audio credits remain in IKEMEN.txt and CONTENT.txt.

Bangers Regular v2.100 is sourced from the Google Fonts repository:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bangers
Its SIL Open Font License is included as BANGERS-OFL.txt.
Black Han Sans is the existing Korean typeface included with this game.
